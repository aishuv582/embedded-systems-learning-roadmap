1. DAC – Digital to Analog Converter

A DAC converts digital data into an analog signal.

Digital → Analog

Example: A microcontroller gives a digital value such as 1010, and the DAC converts it into a corresponding voltage.

Applications:

Audio output
Signal generation
Motor control
Function generators
2. ADC – Analog to Digital Converter

An ADC converts an analog signal into digital data.

Analog → Digital

Example: A temperature sensor produces an analog voltage. The ADC converts that voltage into a digital value that a microcontroller can process.

Applications:

Temperature measurement
Sensors
Battery monitoring
Data acquisition
DAC vs ADC
Feature	DAC	ADC
Full form	Digital to Analog Converter	Analog to Digital Converter
Conversion	Digital → Analog	Analog → Digital
Input	Digital data	Analog voltage/current
Output	Analog signal	Digital data
Example	Audio amplifier output	Temperature sensor reading
