STM32 is a family of 32-bit microcontrollers developed by STMicroelectronics. It is very widely used in embedded systems, automotive electronics, industrial automation, IoT, and consumer electronics.

1. What does STM32 mean?
ST → STMicroelectronics
M → Microcontroller
32 → 32-bit architecture

Most STM32 microcontrollers are based on ARM Cortex-M processors.

2. Why STM32 is important for Embedded Systems

If you are moving toward Embedded Software/Firmware, STM32 is an excellent microcontroller family to learn because you can practice:

Embedded C
GPIO
Timers & counters
Interrupts
ADC/DAC
UART
SPI
I²C
CAN
PWM
DMA
RTOS
Low-power programming
Debugging

4. Important STM32 families
Family	Typical use
STM32F0	Basic/low-cost applications
STM32F1	General-purpose embedded systems
STM32F4	High-performance applications
STM32F7	High-performance systems
STM32G0	Modern general-purpose MCUs
STM32G4	Motor control/power applications
STM32H7	Very high-performance embedded systems
STM32L4/L5	Low-power applications

For learning, STM32F4 or STM32G4 is a good starting point.

5. Tools you should learn
