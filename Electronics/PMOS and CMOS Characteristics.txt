PMOS and CMOS Characteristics
1. PMOS Characteristics

PMOS (P-channel MOSFET) uses a P-type channel. It conducts when the gate voltage is lower than the source voltage.



Main characteristics:

Carrier: Holes
Channel: P-type
Turns ON when: VGS is sufficiently negative
Turns OFF when: VGS ≈ 0
Slower than NMOS because holes have lower mobility.
Higher resistance compared with NMOS for the same dimensions.
Mainly used as the pull-up transistor in CMOS circuits.
2. CMOS Characteristics

CMOS (Complementary MOS) uses both NMOS and PMOS transistors together.



Main characteristics:

Uses complementary NMOS + PMOS.
Very low static power consumption.
High noise immunity.
High input impedance.
High packing density → suitable for VLSI/ICs.
Low power dissipation compared with older logic families.
Good switching performance.
Widely used in microprocessors, microcontrollers, memory, and digital ICs.
PMOS vs CMOS
Characteristic	PMOS	CMOS
Transistors used	PMOS	PMOS + NMOS
Carrier	Holes	Holes + electrons
Speed	Low	High
Power consumption	Higher	Very low static power
Noise immunity	Lower	High
Input impedance	High	Very high
Main use	Pull-up circuits	Digital ICs/VLSI
Density	Lower	Higher
