1. Binary → Decimal

Binary uses only 0 and 1. Each position has a power of 2.

Loading

For example, convert 1011₂ to decimal:

Binary digit	1	0	1	1
Power of 2	2³	2²	2¹	2⁰
Value	8	4	2	1

So:

1011₂ = (1×8) + (0×4) + (1×2) + (1×1)
= 8 + 0 + 2 + 1
= 11₁₀

✅ 1011₂ = 11₁₀

2. Decimal → Binary

Use repeated division by 2 and record the remainders.

Example: Convert 13₁₀ to binary

Division	Quotient	Remainder
13 ÷ 2	6	1
6 ÷ 2	3	0
3 ÷ 2	1	1
1 ÷ 2	0	1

Now read the remainders from bottom to top:

1101

✅ 13₁₀ = 1101₂

Easy trick to remember

Binary → Decimal: Multiply each bit by its power of 2 and add.

Decimal → Binary: Divide by 2 repeatedly → write remainders → read bottom to top
